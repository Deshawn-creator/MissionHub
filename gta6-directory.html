GTA 6 Mission Walkthroughs

Select a mission to open its walkthrough. Early jobs can appear in a different order.

1. Prologue

Follow Lucia through the opening prison sequence and flashback.

How to Start

Begin a new game as Lucia at Hamlet Prison.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Speak to your cellmates, then follow the guard to the counselor’s office.
  2. During the flashback, follow the prompts at the payday loan shop to take the cash and break free when a bystander grabs Lucia.
  3. Run from the police until the scene changes. When you regain control, return to the marked coffee shop.

If you’re stuck

Lucia’s arrest at the coffee shop is scripted. Keep heading toward the marker to finish the prologue.

Source PowerPyx’s Prologue walkthrough

2. Gone Fishing

Recover the floating packages and bring Donnie back to the marina.

How to Start

Begins automatically as Jason after the Prologue.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Follow Donnie’s seaplane by boat. Slow down beside each floating package so Cal can pull it aboard.
  2. Follow the route to the swamp and watch the meeting at the dock through your binoculars. After Donnie is taken, dock and head to the shack at the far end of camp.
  3. Wait for the guard by the entrance to move, then go in and untie Donnie. Get back to the seaplane and fly to Brian’s Marina. Slow down over the water to land and taxi toward the dock.

Optional pickup

Look for the red-and-white trailer on the way to the plane if you want to recover the cash Donnie calls out.

Source PowerPyx’s Gone Fishing walkthrough

3. Gear Up (Pick Up Another Gun)

Buy a longarm and get through the ambush outside the gun shop.

How to Start

Map label · Pick Up Another Gun

Visit the gun shop near Jason’s house in Key Lento after Gone Fishing.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Andrew at the counter and buy a shotgun or tactical rifle. Check your cash before choosing.
  2. When you leave the shop, Kingfishers arrive in a truck. Deal with the ambush and wait for the call that wraps up the mission.

Source PowerPyx’s Gear Up walkthrough

4. Lab Fees (Collect Brian’s Rent)

Collect payment from the boutique and deliver it to Brian’s lockup.

How to Start

Map label · Collect Brian’s Rent

Go to the marked boutique in Key Lento after Gone Fishing. If the door is locked, call Brian.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Speak to the clerk about the rent. Follow the confrontation prompt until he offers the goods in the back room as payment.
  2. Enter 0420 at the lab keypad and pick up the package.
  3. Call Brian again, then go to his lockup. Enter 7700 and place the package in the stash.

Reward

Check the mailbox outside Jason’s house later for $600.

Source PowerPyx’s Lab Fees walkthrough

5. Just a Scratch (Fix Your Car)

Repair and repaint Jason’s Ganado at Joey’s garage.

How to Start

Map label · Fix Your Car

Bring Jason’s ’70 Ganado to Joey’s garage in Key Lento after Gone Fishing.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Joey outside, then drive the Ganado into the garage.
  2. Select Full Engine for the repair. Open Cosmetics → Respray and choose a color to finish.

If you’re stuck

Use Jason’s personal car for this objective. If you destroyed it, saving and reloading can bring it back outside his house.

Source PowerPyx’s Just a Scratch walkthrough

6. Unwanted Attention (Go See Cal)

Retrieve the Phoenix from the impound and deliver it to Brian’s garage.

How to Start

Map label · Go See Cal

Meet Cal at the beach in northern Key Lento after Gone Fishing.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Drive with Cal to the Hamlet impound. Climb the first fence and use the containers at the back to get over the next one.
  2. Look for the red Imponte Phoenix attached to a tow truck. Get into the truck, click the right stick to release the car, then switch to the Phoenix.
  3. Pull up to the exit and wait for the gate to open. Drive out of the flashing search area, then take the Phoenix to the marked Pay ’n’ Spray.
  4. Drop the car at Brian’s garage and collect $150 in the back room. Change your hat and glasses through the item wheel, then buy an outfit at the marked gun shop.

Source PowerPyx’s Unwanted Attention walkthrough

7. Pipework (Collect Mandy’s Rent)

Find the plunger and fix Mandy’s kitchen sink.

How to Start

Map label · Collect Mandy’s Rent

Talk to Mandy outside her Key Lento house after finishing Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch, and Unwanted Attention.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Follow Mandy inside. The plunger is on the bathroom floor across from the bedroom.
  2. Go to the kitchen sink. On Xbox, click the right stick for the plunger prompt, then press RT when prompted to use the wrench.
  3. Finish the conversation. On your way out, you can pick up the Macizo Cigar she throws outside.

Source PowerPyx’s Pipework walkthrough

8. Hey Arnold! (Meet Brian)

Help Brian track down Arnold and follow him to the docks.

How to Start

Map label · Meet Brian

After Pipework, wait for Brian’s call and meet him at the pool bar in southern Key Lento.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Get in Brian’s car and stop at the liquor store to pick up Cal.
  2. Drive toward the pink search area near the radar station. As you get closer, the game pinpoints Arnold’s location.
  3. Follow Arnold by car, then continue on foot when he runs. Catch up to him at the docks and make your choice when he surrenders.

Choice

Letting Arnold go completes the mission.

Source PowerPyx’s Hey Arnold! walkthrough

9. Island Dreams (Work for Kenton)

Dive for three treasures and bring the group back to the boat.

How to Start

Map label · Work for Kenton

Meet Kenton at the Island Dreams shop by the beach after Hey Arnold!

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Take the boat to the dive marker. Put on the yellow scuba gear at the back of the boat and lead the group through the underwater trench.
  2. Equip the metal detector and sweep the seabed outside the wreck. When the light turns green, look down for the pickup icon. Collect all three treasures.
  3. Free Dobbs from the grate and head back to the boat. If you want Dobbs to survive, equip the harpoon and hit the shark twice when it appears.

Reward

$400.

Source PowerPyx’s Island Dreams walkthrough

10. Feng Shui (Meet Cal)

Help Cal rearrange Jason’s house before Lucia comes home.

How to Start

Map label · Meet Cal

Return to Jason’s house after Island Dreams when the mission marker appears.

Mission Walkthrough

  1. Grab a beer from the fridge and sit with Cal.
  2. Help move the TV, coffee table, couch, and chair. If Cal asks about the coffee table again, you can say it’s fine and move on.
  3. Follow the prompts for Tenebrae Lux and Street Trash III. The next morning’s phone call about Lucia’s release closes the mission.

Source PowerPyx’s Feng Shui walkthrough
