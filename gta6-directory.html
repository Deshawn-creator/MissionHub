GTA 6 Mission Walkthroughs

Select a mission to open its walkthrough. Early jobs can appear in a different order.

VantaZero Playthrough Update

Chapter 1 complete · 31% in-game completion

1. Prologue

Start · New game · Hamlet Prison

From the cellblock to the coffee shop

  1. Finish Lucia’s conversation in the cellblock and walk with the guard to her appointment.
  2. At the payday shop in the flashback, respond to the register and grab prompts. Keep moving when the police show up.
  3. After control returns, head straight to the coffee shop marker. The arrest there is part of the story.
2. Gone Fishing

Start · Starts automatically after the Prologue

Pick up the cargo, then get Donnie out

  1. Match Donnie’s flight path in the boat. Ease off the throttle at each package to give Cal time to grab it.
  2. At the swamp, use the binoculars to watch the dock. Once Donnie’s taken, land the boat and work your way to the far shack. Slip in when the doorway is clear.
  3. Take Donnie to the seaplane. Check the red-and-white trailer on the way if you’re collecting the cash. At Brian’s Marina, touch down on the water before taxiing up to the dock.
3. Gear Up (Pick Up Another Gun)

Start · Key Lento gun shop · Opens after Gone Fishing

Buy something before you step outside

  1. Check your balance at Andrew’s counter. Either the shotgun or tactical rifle advances the objective.
  2. The truck outside brings Kingfishers. Clear the encounter, then stay available for the phone call that closes the job.
4. Lab Fees (Collect Brian’s Rent)

Start · Key Lento boutique · Opens after Gone Fishing

Two doors, two codes

  1. Phone Brian if the boutique is locked. Inside, press the clerk about the rent until the back room becomes your next objective.
  2. Use 0420 on the lab door, take the package, and call Brian on the way out.
  3. At the lockup, enter 7700 and put the package away. Jason can check his mailbox later for the $600 payment.
5. Just a Scratch (Fix Your Car)

Start · Joey’s garage in Key Lento · Bring Jason’s ’70 Ganado after Gone Fishing

Finish the engine and the paint

  1. Speak with Joey outside before pulling the Ganado into the shop.
  2. In the garage menu, pick Full Engine. Then open Cosmetics → Respray and apply a color.
  3. If Jason’s car was destroyed, save and reload, then check outside his house for it.
6. Unwanted Attention (Go See Cal)

Start · North Key Lento beach · Meet Cal after Gone Fishing

Get the Phoenix out of impound

  1. Ride with Cal to Hamlet. Go over the outer fence, then use the containers at the back to cross into the yard.
  2. Find the red Phoenix on the tow truck. In the truck, press the right stick to lower it, then move over to the Phoenix.
  3. Wait at the exit gate, clear the flashing search zone, and stop at the marked Pay ’n’ Spray.
  4. Leave the car at Brian’s garage and grab the $150 in back. Use the item wheel to swap Jason’s hat and glasses, then buy clothes at the marked shop.
7. Pipework (Collect Mandy’s Rent)

Start · Mandy’s house in Key Lento · After Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch, and Unwanted Attention

The sink is the job

  1. Go inside with Mandy. Look on the bathroom floor opposite the bedroom for the plunger.
  2. At the kitchen sink on Xbox, press the right stick when the plunger prompt appears, then use RT for the wrench prompt.
  3. Let the conversation finish. The Macizo Cigar Mandy tosses outside is an optional pickup.
8. Hey Arnold! (Meet Brian)

Start · Pool bar in southern Key Lento · Wait for Brian’s call after Pipework

Stay with Arnold until the docks

  1. Take Brian’s car to the liquor store to get Cal. Drive toward the radar station until the pink search zone narrows to Arnold’s spot.
  2. Follow Arnold’s car. When he ditches it, go the rest of the way on foot.
  3. At the docks, the chase ends with a choice. You can let Arnold go and still finish the mission.
9. Island Dreams (Work for Kenton)

Start · Beachside Island Dreams shop · Opens after Hey Arnold!

Sweep the seabed before heading back up

  1. Boat out to the marker. Use the yellow scuba gear at the stern, then swim with the group through the trench.
  2. Scan the sand around the wreck. A green detector light means it’s time to look down for the pickup prompt. You need three finds.
  3. Get Dobbs out from the grate and swim back. To save him when the shark appears, land two harpoon hits. The mission pays $400.
10. Feng Shui (Meet Cal)

Start · Jason’s house · Return once the marker appears after Island Dreams

Set up the house for Lucia

  1. Grab a beer, sit with Cal, and help him shift the TV and furniture. If he circles back to the coffee table, saying it’s fine moves the scene along.
  2. Play through the prompts for Tenebrae Lux and Street Trash III. The release call the next morning wraps up the visit.
11. What’s in the Bag? (Chapter 2)

Start · Starlet Motel · Begins right after Discharge Time

Check underneath the statue

  1. Use Lucia’s inspection controls to rotate the statue until you can read the note on its underside.
  2. Look at the hard drive next. As new jobs open, swing back by Jason’s room for the ongoing Take Care of Jason objective.
12. Overnight Charges (Chapter 2)

Start · Starlet Motel reception · Available after inspecting the bag

Find out what Linda wants

  1. Leave the room and head to the front desk. Talk to Linda about what’s owed.
  2. If you picked up Lucia’s necklace in Chapter 1, the scene uses it as collateral. You can reclaim it when you settle the $900 balance in Behind on Payments.
13. Just Moved In (Chapter 2)

Start · Alpha convenience store across from the Starlet Motel

Clear the shopping list

  1. Work along the shelves until you’ve collected all eight marked items. Check the minimap if a marker is still showing.
  2. Each pickup rings up on its own. Once the last item registers, head back to the motel room.
14. Girl’s Trip (Chapter 2)

Start · Starlet Motel · After Overnight Charges and Just Moved In

Take care of Jason before going into town

  1. Ask around the motel for pills and head to Vicki’s room, number 10. Bring what she gives you back to Jason before returning to her.
  2. Ride into town with Vicki and follow the stops through Delights to the boatyard and car wash.
  3. Get through the Kolts encounter and use the shutter prompt. The outing ends at Dunk’s trailer, where the lead on stronger pills begins.
15. Moses (Chapter 2)

Start · Moses’s garage · Bring a car after Girl’s Trip

Pick a car you can keep

  1. In the yard, point the Waink scanner at each of the three practice cars and check what it reports about their trackers.
  2. Complete the guided interaction at the Montagne XL and go back to Moses.
  3. Register an untracked car as Lucia’s personal vehicle. Scanning the car you bring before you leave saves a trip if it has a tracker.
16. New Look (Chapter 2)

Start · Port Gellhorn hair salon · Opens after Girl’s Trip

Get Lucia a haircut

  1. Open the style menu with the salon clerk and buy any available cut.
  2. This clears the salon objective. The clothing objective has its own marker and purchase.
17. How Do I Look? (Chapter 2)

Start · Bargain World · South Port Gellhorn

Buy both pieces of the outfit

  1. Pick any top, then buy pants or shorts for Lucia. The objective clears once you’ve made a purchase in both categories.
18. Ambulance Chaser (Chapter 2)

Start · Barn east of the Starlet Motel · Use the rear stairs

Follow the ambulance to the crash

  1. When the ambulance pulls away, take the ATV from the barn. Stay close and let the chase play out until the wreck.
  2. Complete the marked interaction at the ambulance and leave once the game clears the objective.
19. Behind on Payments (Chapter 2)

Start · Starlet Motel reception · Bring $900

Settle the room bill

  1. Keep doing open story jobs until Lucia has the full amount. Moses can also buy eligible vehicles once his introduction is complete.
  2. Pay Linda at the desk. If she took the necklace earlier, this is where you get it back.
20. Slam Dunk (Chapter 2)

Start · Dunk’s Port Gellhorn trailer · Wait for the follow-up call

Bring the painkillers back to Jason

  1. Meet Dunk and follow him to Dr. Bill. At the docks, stay behind the escort until you reach the office.
  2. When the meeting turns hostile, get out of the compound and shake the chase. Pick up Bill again before you head to the airstrip.
  3. Clear the airstrip encounter and look for Dunk in the central building. Drop him at his trailer to trigger the scene where Lucia brings the pills back to Jason.
  4. If Dunk hasn’t called back, check Map → Story for Painkiller Supplier and work through the other open Chapter 2 jobs.
